// About Sai Siddhi Enterprises

We're not a holding company. We're the first call when something needs fixing.

Sai Siddhi Enterprises is the enterprise group behind a small number of focused, specialist businesses. We back each one with capital, experience and an operating team — not a hands-off cheque and a logo on a slide.

// Who we are

One group, built one venture at a time.

Rather than spreading thin across many unrelated businesses, SSE backs a small number of ventures properly — deeply enough to matter, patiently enough to let each one build a real reputation in its own market.

SRC Technologies, our group's smart-systems business, is the clearest example: built under SSE, now growing at a scale of its own.
